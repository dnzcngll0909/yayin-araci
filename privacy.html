Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Yayin Araci is a private tool used only by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own YouTube channels and social media accounts.

  • Data accessed: only the owner's own channels, via the permissions the owner grants (video upload and basic channel/video statistics).
  • Storage: access tokens are stored only on the owner's local computer. No data is stored on any server.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared with or transferred to third parties.
  • Other users: the tool does not collect data from viewers or any other person.
  • Revoking access: access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and to the YouTube Terms of Service.
